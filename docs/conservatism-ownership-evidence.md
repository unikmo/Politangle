# Conservatism × ownership — evidence decision

Status: evidence-informed validation prior; not psychometrically calibrated.

## Decision

The Conservatism family must not assign `0` relevance to the ownership construct.

Canonical prior:

- construct: `ownership`
- relevance: `1` (relevant / typical, not defining)
- direction: `+1` (toward predominantly private ownership, with regulation and social obligations compatible)

This means substantial social/public/cooperative ownership creates some tension with the broad Conservative family, but it cannot by itself erase an otherwise strongly conservative profile.

Public provision, universal healthcare, redistribution and regulation remain distinct constructs. Supporting them does not automatically count against Conservatism.

## Evidence

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Conservatism

`SEP-CONSERVATISM`

The current SEP entry describes a conservative organic social vision as skeptical of large-scale planning and as placing faith in family, private property and religion. It also stresses conservatism's particularism and diversity rather than equating Conservatism with laissez-faire economics.

Therefore private property is relevant to broad Conservatism, but the evidence does not justify making it an invariant or defining (`2`) criterion.

### Cambridge University Press — Christian Democracy / Social Capitalism

`CAMBRIDGE-CHRISTIAN-DEMOCRACY`

Christian democracy is an important conservative-adjacent subtype and explicitly combines private property and a market economy with welfare, distributive justice, solidarism and a social-market / third-way conception.

This is further evidence against both extremes:

- `0` would wrongly imply ownership is irrelevant to Conservatism;
- `2` would wrongly collapse Conservatism into laissez-faire or libertarian capitalism.

### Chapel Hill Expert Survey

`CHES-ECON-GALTAN`

Comparative party research operationalizes the economic left-right dimension using issues including privatization, taxation, regulation, spending and welfare. This supports treating ownership/privatization as a genuine political-economic discriminator, while not assuming it uniquely defines a political family.

## Red Team

Potential error: 'Conservatives favor private property, therefore public healthcare or strong regulation should lower the Conservative score.'

Rejected. Ownership, public provision, redistribution and regulation are separate constructs. Private ownership can coexist with extensive welfare and regulation, especially in Christian-democratic and one-nation/paternalistic conservative traditions.

Potential error: 'Private property is mentioned in conservative theory, therefore ownership should have relevance 2.'

Rejected. Conservatism is historically and cross-nationally diverse; its more defining features in the current broad-family model remain continuity/tradition and cautious change. Ownership is retained at relevance 1 pending empirical calibration.

## Validation requirement

The `1` relevance is an expert prior. Pilot data must test sensitivity, item discrimination and cross-national behavior before Politangle makes strong psychometric claims.
