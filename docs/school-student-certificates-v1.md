# Politangle School Student Certificates v1

Status: **institutional classroom feature.**

## Purpose

The certificate recognises completion of a Politangle Political Literacy Classroom Programme. It does not certify a student's political orientation, belief answers, personality, or civic virtue.

## Teacher workflow

1. A licensed teacher runs an anonymous classroom room or creates a classroom assessment/activity.
2. From the classroom screen, the teacher types one completed student's name per line. The room permits up to the number of joined participants; an assessment permits up to its configured participant count.
3. Politangle opens a print-ready document with one certificate per student. The teacher can print it or use the browser's Save as PDF option.
4. The teacher gives the certificates to the students.

Student names are used only to generate the immediate print document. They are not stored in Politangle and are never linked to anonymous classroom answers.

## Certificate wording

> **Certificate of Classroom Learning**
>
> This certificate is presented to **[Student name]** for completing the Politangle Political Literacy Classroom Programme.
>
> The programme supports informed, respectful discussion of political ideas. It does not report, assess or label personal political beliefs.

## Commercial rule

Classroom certificates are included in the school's institutional licence. Students do not pay an individual certificate fee. The separate paid adult Political Literacy Certificate remains a voluntary 18+ independent assessment.
