# MBTI and political angles — research protocol (exploratory)

## Question
Do self-reported MBTI categories have reproducible associations with any of Politangle's eight political dimensions, after accounting for age, country, education, and other plausible confounders?

## Status
No participant dataset has been collected or analyzed. No association, significance, predictive power, or causal effect is established. Do not display MBTI-to-politics predictions in individual results.

## Study design
1. Recruit consenting adults (18+) across countries and political viewpoints. Keep this separate from school and minor-facing flows.
2. Ask participants to optionally self-report MBTI type and whether it came from a formal assessment or a casual online quiz. Prefer an independently validated continuous personality inventory as a comparison measure.
3. Administer the current versioned Politangle questionnaire and record eight axis scores, missingness, country, age band, and optional education. Minimize personal data; publish retention and withdrawal rules.
4. Preregister eight-dimensional outcomes, exclusion rules, sample-size/power calculation, primary comparisons, and multiple-testing adjustment before inspecting associations.
5. Estimate associations using descriptive plots, confidence intervals, adjusted regression and appropriate multiplicity correction. Assess reliability, country heterogeneity, and out-of-sample prediction against simple baselines.
6. Conduct sensitivity checks for self-selection, response style, questionnaire language, and differences between self-reported and assessed MBTI.
7. Publish null results and uncertainty. Do not imply MBTI determines ideology, or that correlation validates either instrument.

## Release gates
- Independent ethics/privacy and measurement review.
- No tracking or profiling of students; no political-personality inference about individuals.
- No use of MBTI for scoring, ranking, persuasion, targeting, or access decisions.
- Any eventual public summary must describe aggregate evidence and its limitations; no invented matches.
